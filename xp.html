HOW XP WORKS

No wallet.
No nonsense.

XP is an off-chain community progression system. It is not a cryptocurrency, has no cash value, and does not promise financial rewards.

+100Play

Show up and play

Play a GankByte game or take part in an organised gaming session.

+100Help

Make it better

Report a useful bug, test a build, answer a question, or help another player or developer.

+500Build

Ship something

Submit an accepted project, useful tool, guide, asset, or community contribution.

XP is awarded manually for verified community contributions. The database records approved awards so the system stays transparent and auditable.
