04 // PROGRESSION

Play more.
Build more.

GankByte XP recognises the people who play, create, test, explain, and help make the community better.

XP V0.1

No wallet.
No nonsense.

XP is an off-chain community progression system. It is not a cryptocurrency, it has no cash value, and it is not a promise of future rewards.

+100Play

Show up and play

Take part in a GankByte game, community challenge, or organised gaming session.

+100Help

Make it better

Report a useful bug, test a build, answer a question, or help another player or developer.

+500Build

Ship something

Submit an accepted project, useful tool, guide, asset, or community contribution.

For now, XP is awarded manually for verified community contributions. We will add accounts and a shared leaderboard only when the system is ready to be fair and useful.
