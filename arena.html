HOW TO PLAY

Move fast.
Think faster.

Collect the GankBytes to score and build your combo. Rare bonuses appear during the run with powerful but temporary perks. Glitches now spread out and gain new movement styles as the waves rise.

  • Shield — Absorbs one hit.
  • Overdrive — Doubles collection score for six seconds.
  • Time Shard — Adds eight seconds.
  • Boost Charge — Adds one dash, up to five total.

The arena wraps around at every edge. Purple diamonds chase, purple triangles flank, and purple sharp shapes sprint. Power-ups use light blue, orange, and red.
