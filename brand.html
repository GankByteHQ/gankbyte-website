Use contrast, grids, compact labels, deliberate glitches, and a little humour. Avoid generic crypto or corporate fintech styling.

Palette

Lime #C6FF3D is the action colour. Purple #9A7BFF signals Arena/glitches. Orange #FF855C signals heat and warnings. Base surfaces are #0A0B0F, #121419, and #191B20.

Type

Use bold Arial/Helvetica for the current system. Headlines are large, tight, and confident. Supporting copy stays calm, readable, and muted. Labels are uppercase with generous letter spacing.

Image rules

Prefer original mascot art, abstract game shapes, screenshots, interface details, and real community moments. Do not use copyrighted characters, official logos, or stolen game assets.

Voice

Funny, direct, slightly stupid on purpose, developer-friendly, and honest about what is live. Say “we are testing” when it is testing. Never fake hype or promise financial outcomes.
