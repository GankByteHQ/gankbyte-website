FIELD GUIDE // SIGNAL SWARM

Save the swarm.
Use the right skill.

Signals walk by themselves. You do not steer them directly. Select a skill, then click the Signal, corruption, or gap where you want to intervene.

RedirectTurn a nearby Signal around.
BridgeBuild across a gap or over a red hazard.
CleanseRemove nearby corruption or red hazards.
PulseOverclock a Signal or the whole swarm.
RecallReturn nearby Signals to safety.
BlockerStop one Signal so the others turn around.
FloaterGive a Signal a parachute for safer drops.

First move: let the Signals fall onto the lower platforms, then select Bridge and click the large gap before they reach it. Rescue them at the green exit. Use Safe speed while learning, then Push or Gank for higher scores.

Keyboard: 1–7 select a skill, Space uses it at the last target, Escape pauses, and R restarts.

HOW TO PLAY

Guide the swarm.
Do not control it.

Signals enter from the hatch, walk automatically, fall when the terrain drops, and leave through the exit. This is a Lemmings-style rescue game: you intervene at the right moment instead of steering a character directly.

  • Redirect turns the nearest Signal around when a route becomes dangerous.
  • Bridge builds a temporary platform across a gap or over a hazard.
  • Cleanse removes nearby corruption and red hazards.
  • Pulse makes a Signal or the whole swarm faster for a scoring window.
  • Recall returns nearby Signals to their last safe platform with a small score penalty.
  • Blocker stops one Signal so the following Signals turn around.
  • Floater gives one Signal a parachute for safer drops.

Runner Signals are fast and fragile. Carrier Signals are slower but worth more. Spark Signals can repair a nearby corruption zone as they pass.
