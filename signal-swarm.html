FIELD GUIDE // SIGNAL SWARM

Save the swarm.
Use the right skill.

Signals walk by themselves. Select one limited skill, then click an individual Signal to assign it. They turn at red glitches and active Blockers, fall when a platform ends, and can only survive the route you build.

ClimberLets a Signal climb a vertical wall instead of turning around.
FloaterOpens a parachute so a long fall is survivable.
BomberStarts a countdown, then removes terrain and the Signal.
BlockerStops a Signal and turns followers around for about 7 seconds.
BuilderLays a staircase across gaps and up to higher routes.
BasherBreaks through a wall in the Signal's direction.
MinerDigs a diagonal tunnel through the platform ahead.
DiggerDigs straight down through the platform below.

First move: start the run, wait for a Signal to land, choose Builder, then click that Signal. Place skills on the front of the stream to open a route while Blockers turn the Signals behind them. Rescue the minimum goal, then let the round finish so every rescue adds to the score.

Keyboard: 1-8 select a skill, Space assigns it to the selected Signal, Escape pauses, and R restarts.

HOW TO PLAY

Guide the swarm.
Build the route.

Signals enter from the hatch and walk automatically. Select a skill, then click one Signal to assign it. They turn around at red glitches and active Blockers, fall from unfinished platforms, and leave through the gate when you create a safe route.

  • Climber climbs a vertical wall instead of turning around.
  • Floater survives a long drop with a parachute.
  • Bomber counts down, removes nearby terrain, and sacrifices the Signal.
  • Blocker stops one Signal and reverses followers for about 7 seconds.
  • Builder lays a staircase across gaps and toward higher platforms.
  • Basher breaks through a wall in the Signal's direction.
  • Miner cuts a diagonal tunnel through the platform ahead.
  • Digger removes the platform below and starts a controlled drop.

Runner Signals are fast and fragile. Carrier Signals are slower but worth more. Spark Signals are the most valuable route-repair specialists.
