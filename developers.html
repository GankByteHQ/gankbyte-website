Code, chaos,
and useful stuff.

GankByte Labs is a home for practical experiments, community tools, Lua projects, and developers who want to make things people actually use.

Open direction

Tools & prototypes

Small utilities, experiments, and game-adjacent projects. Build in public, keep the scope honest, and improve the thing after someone uses it.

  • Lua and gameplay experiments
  • Community tools and automations
  • Playtest utilities
  • Documentation that people can understand
Coming later

Developer projects

As the community grows, developers can propose projects, collaborate with testers, and share work through the GankByte ecosystem.

  • Project showcases
  • Build competitions
  • Feedback and bug reports
  • Resources where legally permitted

THE STANDARD

Make it
real.

No pretending a concept is a finished product. No claiming partnerships that do not exist. Show the build, explain the limits, and let people try it.

GankByte can support Lua and FiveM-related work, but every project must respect the rules, licences, and intellectual property of the platforms and games involved.
