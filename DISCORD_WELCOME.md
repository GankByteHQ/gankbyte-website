# Welcome to GankByte

GankByte is a gaming community and developer ecosystem first.

Play the games, share clips and memes, talk PC gaming, test builds, break Lua scripts, and help decide what gets built next.

Start here:

- Read the community [rules](https://gankbyte.com/rules.html).
- Play [Byte Rush](https://gankbyte.com/arena.html?v=18).
- Try [Glitch Dash](https://gankbyte.com/glitch-dash.html?v=6).
- Share feedback or report a bug.
- Say hello and tell us what you play.

No token shilling, fake hype, or fake partnerships. The product is the games, community, and things we build together.
