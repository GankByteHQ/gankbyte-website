FIELD GUIDE // PACKET SIEGE

Hold the core.
Push the score.

Packets descend in a moving formation. Shoot them before they reach the core, then survive the next wave. Enemies that get closer are worth more, so skilled players can take a controlled risk for a higher score.

BasicOne hit, reliable points.
FastQuick and worth more.
TankNeeds several hits.
ShooterFires back at the cannon.
SplitterBreaks into fragments.
BossArrives every fifth wave.

Power-ups: Rapid Fire, Pierce, Lightning, Shield, Bomb, and Overdrive. Move with A/D or the arrow keys. Space shoots. On a phone, drag the board to move and tap to shoot.

HOW TO PLAY

Protect the core.
One more wave.

Shoot the descending packets, collect power-ups, and keep enemies away from the core. The deeper a packet reaches, the more score it gives, but it also becomes a bigger threat.

  • Move and shoot Use A/D, arrows, or the touch controls.
  • Build a combo Consecutive kills raise your multiplier to x12.
  • Take the risk Close packets pay more, but damage the core if they break through.
  • Defeat the boss Every fifth wave introduces a heavy boss with a unique attack pattern.
