Contribution standard

Share responsibly

Only publish scripts, assets, and resources you created or have permission to share. Keep dependencies clear and document what the resource does.

  • No copied or proprietary game files
  • No Rockstar, server, or creator assets without permission
  • Document dependencies and permissions
  • Use a clear licence before release
