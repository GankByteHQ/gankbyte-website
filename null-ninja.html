FIELD GUIDE // NULL NINJA

Move fast.
Strike clean.

Null runs automatically. Use the same core keyboard layout as Stick Fighter: move, jump, punch, kick, dash, and trigger your charged ability when ready.

MoveA/D or left/right adjusts your run line.
JumpW or the Jump button jumps. Press again in the air for a double jump.
PunchF or the Punch button makes a quick close-range strike.
KickG or the Kick button reaches farther and uses a real kicking pose.
DashSpace or Shift bursts forward and can cut through danger.
FlowPress E at 100% for a short protected score surge. S or Down slides under low threats.

Mobile: use the buttons below the board. The run stays automatic so your attention stays on timing.

HOW TO PLAY

Run the route.
Own the chain.

The network scrolls forward automatically. Read the next threat, choose the clean action, and keep the combo alive long enough to reach Gank Mode.

  • Jump hazards Clear gaps, spikes, and low barriers.
  • Strike enemies Normal, shielded, flying, and heavy drones reward different timing.
  • Chase perfects Counter and dash attacks give more score and Flow.
  • Stay alive Three hits end the run, so risk is valuable only when it is clean.
