HOW IT WORKS

Thirty levels.
Three bosses.

The campaign starts with basic passwords and ends with systems that mix binary, syntax checks, logic traps, and memory pressure. Every tenth level is a boss firewall with three stages.

School computer to gaming server

Basic code, passwords, and simple decoding.

04-09

Web to satellite systems

Binary, patterns, database logic, and shorter timers.

10

Three linked security gates that demand clean answers under pressure.

11-19

Central system to root access

More deceptive instructions, faster challenge swaps, and tougher logic.

20

A multi-stage system collapse that rewards a careful route.

21-30

Endgame and mainframe

Randomised challenge rules, very short timers, and the final mainframe fight.
