FIELD GUIDE // BYTE STACK

Stack clean.
Gank the clear.

Move and rotate the falling blocks to fill a complete line. A clear gives points and charge; consecutive clears build the combo. The stack speeds up as your level rises.

MoveUse A/D or left/right arrows, or the touch buttons.
RotateUse W, Up, X, or Rotate to turn the active block.
DropS/down soft drops. Space or Hard drop locks it instantly.
HoldPress C to save the active block for later once per turn.
GlitchesFrom level 3, special blocks can explode or chain when cleared.
Byte BombFill the meter, then remove a selected board area with the power button.

Mobile: use the controls below the board. Tap Rotate, swipe-friendly movement buttons, and Hard drop keep the board playable with one hand.

HOW TO PLAY

Know the shape.
Chase the chain.

Byte Stack starts with familiar falling blocks, then adds controlled glitches and a Byte meter. Clear safely or hold the stack for a larger clear when the risk is worth it.

  • Clear lines Complete a row to remove it and score.
  • Build combos Consecutive clears pay more.
  • Watch the top The run ends when a new block cannot enter.
  • Use Byte power Spend a full meter to break a dangerous setup.
