HOW TO PLAY

Read the gap.
Make the move.

Every incoming gate leaves one safe lane. Switch into it before the gate reaches you, collect power-ups from the safe lane, and keep your streak alive as the speed rises.

  • Move — Use W/S or Up/Down to switch lanes.
  • Dash — Press Space to become invulnerable briefly.
  • Power-ups — Shield absorbs a hit, Overdrive doubles score, and Phase resets Dash while granting brief protection.
  • Speed — Every five clean gates increase the speed level.
  • Endless run — There is no countdown. Keep going until all three lives are gone.
