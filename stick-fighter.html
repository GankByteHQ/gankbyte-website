FIELD GUIDE // STICK FIGHTER

Read the hit.
Own the moment.

Defeat every stick in the level to move forward. Your health, stamina, and focus reset at the start of each level, so every fight begins clean.

MoveA/D move. W jumps.
PunchF is quick and short. Use it when you are close.
KickG reaches farther and hits harder, but is slower.
BlockHold S. A well-timed block prevents damage and builds focus.
DashSpace bursts through space and gives a brief dodge window.
SpecialE fires a charged strike. Land hits and blocks to fill focus.
Campaign20 levels add smarter rivals, bosses, and two-on-one fights. There is no timer.

Local two-player: Player 1 uses A/D, W, S, F, G, Space, and E. Player 2 uses the arrow keys, Numpad 1/2, Numpad 3/4/5/6.

HOW TO PLAY

Make space.
Clear the level.

Every action has a tell. Keep your footing, watch the rival's reach, and save the special for the opening that matters.

  • Read distance Punch up close; kick when the rival leaves a gap.
  • Use the guard Blocking is strongest when you meet the attack, not when you hold it forever.
  • Build focus Clean hits and perfect blocks charge the special.
  • Gank the level Dash behind a rival, land the special, and turn one opening into a finish.
