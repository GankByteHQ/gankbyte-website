# GankByte Weekend Challenge #001

## Byte Rush high-score hunt

Challenge is live.

Play Byte Rush, chase your highest score, and share the result in Discord.

**How to enter**

1. Open https://gankbyte.com/arena.html?v=18
2. Sign in with Discord if you want your score on the public leaderboard.
3. Complete your run.
4. Share a screenshot or clip in Discord.
5. Submit the proof link on https://gankbyte.com/xp.html for the base `+100 XP` award.

**Format**

- Game: Byte Rush
- Attempts: repeatable solo runs
- Winner: highest verified or automatically published score during the event window
- Prize: bragging rights and community recognition

Play fair. No scripts, bots, modified clients, fake screenshots, or score manipulation.
